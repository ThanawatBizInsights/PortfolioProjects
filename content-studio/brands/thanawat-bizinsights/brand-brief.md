# Brand brief: Thanawat BizInsights (PROPOSED, needs owner confirmation)

> **Status: proposal.** There was no brand guide in the repo. This brief was put together from the only material available: the GitHub account name `ThanawatBizInsights` and the two portfolio projects (`COVID PortfolioProject.sql`, SQL data exploration; `Movie Correlation Project.ipynb`, pandas data cleaning and correlation analysis of budget vs. gross).
> Everything marked **ASSUMPTION** is a guess for the owner to confirm or change.

## What the material shows (facts)
- The owner does hands-on data analysis: SQL (filtering, aggregation, data type fixes, joins over COVID deaths and vaccinations data) and Python/pandas (cleaning, type conversion, duplicates, correlation, scatter plots).
- The account name pairs a personal name with "BizInsights", so the focus is turning data into business insight.

## Positioning (ASSUMPTION)
Explain data and AI in plain Thai, so small business owners, online sellers and office workers make better decisions from their own numbers, without needing a statistics degree.

## Audience (ASSUMPTION)
1. Thai online sellers and small business owners who run their own Facebook/IG ads and read their own sales numbers.
2. Thai office workers who use Excel or Google Sheets and want to use AI in real work.
3. Students and career-switchers interested in data analysis (portfolio-adjacent).

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Example | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Data sense: อ่านตัวเลขไม่หลงทาง | correlation vs causation, misleading averages | 35% |
| AI at work: real workflows | let AI draft a sales summary, then check the numbers | 25% |
| Sheets / SQL quick tips | one formula or query per video | 15% |
| Behind the scenes (real projects only) | how the movie-budget analysis was done | 15% |
| Promotional | **only if real services or products exist (ASSUMPTION: none yet)** | 10% |

## Voice
- Friendly, practical, honest. Like a data-savvy friend, not a lecturer.
- **Ending particles: none (ASSUMPTION).** Neutral casual Thai with no ครับ/ค่ะ. Soft particles (นะ, เลย) are OK sparingly. Change this to ครับ or ค่ะ if the owner prefers; the writer will apply it consistently.
- Uses English data terms people actually say (data, correlation, dashboard, AI, prompt) with a one-line Thai explanation.
- Never shows invented numbers as real ones; illustrative data is always labelled `ตัวเลขสมมติ`.

## Visual identity (PROPOSED, defined in `theme.json`)
- Deep navy background (#0E1A2B → #1B2D45) with a subtle grid ("data paper")
- Amber accent #FFB627 (highlights, key phrase marker), teal #3DD6C4 (second data series, mascot), coral #FF6B6B for "wrong idea" strike-throughs
- Fonts: **Prompt** (headings) and **Noto Sans Thai** (body). Both support Thai and are under the SIL OFL.
- **Mascot (PROPOSED):** a small teal "chart tile" character with a bar-chart belly, drawn in code (an original, owned asset). Reactions: idle, think (?), surprised (!), happy. It can be turned off in `theme.json`.

## Words to avoid
ในยุคดิจิทัล, พลิกโฉม, ยกระดับ, ห้ามพลาด, ปัง, รวยไว, "guaranteed result" claims, and any promise of sales outcomes.
