# Content planning

## Per idea (fill these in `package.md`)
1. **Audience**: one specific group (e.g. "แม่ค้าออนไลน์ที่ยิงแอดเอง", not "ทุกคน").
2. **One takeaway**: one Thai sentence the viewer should remember.
3. **Three hooks** using different mechanisms, then pick one:
   - question that challenges an assumption
   - surprising contrast / everyday analogy
   - relatable problem ("ใครเคย…")
   - bold-but-true statement
   - show the result or demo first
   The pick must be understandable with sound off within 2–3 s, and the video must pay it off.
4. **One main idea per short video.** Extra points become future posts.
5. **One CTA** matching the objective: save (useful reference), share (helps a friend), comment (genuine question), follow (series), product view (real product only).

## Monthly mix (default, adjust per brand brief)
| Type | Share |
|---|---|
| Useful tips / explainers | ~35% |
| Demonstrations of real workflows | ~25% |
| Relatable problems | ~15% |
| Behind the scenes (real work only) | ~15% |
| Promotional (real offers only) | ~10% |

## AI education topics
Demonstrate actual workflows (real inputs, real outputs, including the bits you had to fix). Tool features, prices and limits change, so verify them against the official source on the production date and record the URL and check date in the package.

## Facebook vs Instagram
Same core video, different captions. Facebook rewards a slightly longer conversational explanation and a discussion prompt. Instagram needs the hook in line 1, tighter copy, and hashtags at the end. Never paste one caption into both.
